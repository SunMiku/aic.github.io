    Welcome Message

    Dear colleagues,

    We are thrilled to welcome you to the 1st International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Computing (AIC 2026), taking place from April 21 to 25, 2026, in Kaifeng, China.

    AIC 2026 serves as a high-level international forum where innovative academics and industrial experts come together to present their latest research advances in the fields of artificial intelligence and computing. Our conference brings together leading researchers and practitioners from around the world to explore groundbreaking developments across a wide spectrum of AI and computing disciplines.

    The conference features an exceptional program including keynote speeches by world-renowned scholars, regular sessions covering broad technical areas, and special sessions focusing on popular topics. Our comprehensive scope encompasses everything from artificial intelligence and machine learning theory to intelligent manufacturing, computer vision, and cryptographic applications. Conference papers will be forwarded for publication consideration in JCR Q2 journals.

    We look forward to welcoming you to AIC 2026 in Kaifeng, China, for what promises to be an enlightening and productive conference experience.

    Warm regards,

    Conference Committee General Chair, AIC2026
