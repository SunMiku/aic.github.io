    2025 Intelligent Scheduling and Artificial Intelligence Technical Forum

    Lingnan University, Hong Kong
    Date: December 23 to 26, 2025

    The 2025 Intelligent Scheduling and Artificial Intelligence Technical Forum is a premier international conference focused on the intersection of intelligent scheduling systems and artificial intelligence technologies. The forum will be held at Lingnan University, Hong Kong, during December 23-26, 2025. The 2025 ISAI Technical Forum aims to provide a high-level international platform for distinguished academics, industry leaders, and researchers to present their cutting-edge advances in intelligent scheduling algorithms, AI-driven optimization, and automated decision-making systems.

    This forum serves as a vital venue for scholarly exchange, technological innovation, and cross-sector collaboration. The forum will bring together experts from academia and industry to explore the latest developments in intelligent scheduling applications across various domains, including manufacturing, transportation, healthcare, and service industries. Participants will have opportunities to share breakthrough research, establish strategic partnerships, and discuss future trends in AI-powered scheduling solutions.

    The conference will be featured by keynote speeches given by world-renowned scholars, regular sessions with broad coverage, and special sessions focusing on key topics.

    Key Topics:

    • evolutionary computation theory and applications
    • multi-objective optimization for scheduling problems
    • hybrid intelligent algorithms
    • machine learning-based scheduling systems
    • real-world implementations of AI-driven scheduling solutions across various industrial sectors
